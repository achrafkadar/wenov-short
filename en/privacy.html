Privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026. Last updated: October 19, 2026.

Wenov Short, a Wenov service, complies with Quebec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25). This policy explains, in plain terms, what information we collect on short.wenov.ca, why, and what your rights are. The French version is the reference version.

Person in charge of the protection of personal information

Achraf Kadar, Wenov

Email: ads@wenov.ca

Information we collect

Through the booking form, only what you give us:

  • your first name and email;
  • your Instagram, TikTok or website, if you provide it;
  • the package you choose and, for Carte blanche, your project options.

We also record the page you came from and advertising campaign parameters (for example utm_source), to know which ad brought you here.

Why we use it

  • To answer you and deliver the service you asked for.
  • To measure how well our ads perform, only if you accept cookies.

We do not sell your information or use it for any other purpose without your consent.

Cookies and ad measurement

Only with your consent, the page loads the Meta pixel (Facebook and Instagram), which measures visits and requests coming from our ads. It is off by default. You can change your choice at any time with the “Manage cookies” link at the bottom of the offer page, or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Service providers and transfers outside Quebec

To work, the page relies on providers that may process or store information outside Quebec, including in the United States:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the page;
  • FormSubmit forwards the form to our inbox;
  • Google Workspace hosts our email;
  • Google Fonts provides the typeface;
  • Meta provides the measurement pixel, if you accept it.

These providers only access the information they need to deliver their service.

Retention

We keep your requests for 24 months after our last exchange, then delete them, unless a legal obligation requires us to keep them longer.

Your rights

You can ask to access your information, have it corrected or deleted, and withdraw your consent. Write to ads@wenov.ca. We reply within 30 days. You can also file a complaint with the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

Security and incidents

The page is served over HTTPS and access to our inboxes is protected. If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious injury, we notify the people concerned and the Commission d’accès à l’information.

Changes

We will update this policy as needed. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.
